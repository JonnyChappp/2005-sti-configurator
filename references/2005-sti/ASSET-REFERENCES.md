These are the reference galleries and primary document images used to build the configurator's matching showcase assets. Inspect each selected photograph; auction cars often have changes that are easy to mistake for original equipment.

## Authoritative visual documents

- [U.S. sales brochure](sources/2005-us-impreza-brochure.pdf): original exterior, interior, paint matrix, generic accessory illustrations. Check trim labels before using an accessory illustration.
- [Color matrix image](sources/brochure-page-24.png): the STI row establishes its five colors.
- [Global accessory application table, first page](sources/global-page-13.png) and [second page](sources/global-page-14.png): useful part identity and foreign-market distinctions. Do not import its whole assortment as U.S. options.
- [Later U.S. performance catalog](sources/2011-us-performance-parts.pdf): useful gauge and part photographs, with the explicit limitation that a later catalog is not proof of original-year availability.

## Produced showcase assets

1. Thirty consistent exterior views: five paints × gold/silver BBS × front/side/rear.
2. Three matching interior views: front cockpit, full overhead cabin and rear seating.
3. Thirteen accessory-card images, including the corrected blue/no-logo J5010SS700 mat reconstruction.

Accessory selections are represented in cards and pricing but are not yet composited into the vehicle or cabin showcase images.

## Avoid these visual substitutions

- 2004 dashboard, console or narrower BBS wheels; 2006–07 hawkeye front end or later roof spoiler.
- Generic WRX hood scoop, bumper, wheels or five-speed gear pattern.
- JDM right-hand-drive cabins or special-edition interiors.
- Aftermarket lowering, front-mount intercoolers, wing stabilizers, mudflaps or nonstandard exhausts copied from auction galleries.
- A modern black STI-logo mat passed off as the late-2004 blue mat without checking its original appearance.
- A new paint code inferred from an arbitrary on-screen hexadecimal value. Metallic and pearl materials require light-response matching, not just a flat swatch.

Production-use permissions and source provenance should be recorded per chosen asset. These links establish research references, not a completed asset license inventory.
